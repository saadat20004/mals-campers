Good to know

Questions, answered.

Yes. Both trailers can be delivered, leveled and set up at your campground, including The Gorge, the Washington State Horse Park, Lake Easton and many more. Delivery is priced by distance, so add your location to the request and Mal will quote it.

A vehicle rated for the trailer weight with a 7 pin connector. The receiver and brake controller are provided. The Jay Flight weighs 8,180 lb (775 lb hitch) and the Coleman 4,840 lb (550 lb hitch). Mileage is unlimited.

Your food and personal items. The kitchens come with cookware, dishes and utensils. The Jay Flight includes linens, camp chairs and an outdoor rug. For the Coleman, bedding is available on request, otherwise bring bedding, pillows and towels.

The Jay Flight has an optional generator with up to 4 hours of use per day, which is great for dry camping. The Coleman does not include a generator and works best at sites with a 30 amp hookup.

No. Both trailers are pet free and no smoking or vaping of any kind is allowed inside. A cleaning fee applies if this policy is broken.

The Jay Flight uses a moderate cancellation policy and the Coleman uses a flexible one. Mal will share the exact terms when confirming your booking.
